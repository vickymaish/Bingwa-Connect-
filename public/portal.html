Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How long until I’m reconnected?

The portal updates your account when it receives payment confirmation. Keep this page open while it checks. If no confirmation appears within two minutes, check your M-Pesa message and contact support.

What if I paid but I’m still offline?

Keep your M-Pesa confirmation message and contact PigAbiz support using the number or WhatsApp link above. The portal records your account status; network equipment may still need attention.

Can I pay for someone else?

Choose the plan for the account and enter the phone number linked to that account. The M-Pesa prompt is sent to the number you enter, so the person with that phone must approve it.
