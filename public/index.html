How fast am I reconnected?

Usually within a minute of approving the M-Pesa prompt. If Safaricom is slow to confirm, it can take a few minutes. Keep this page open and it will tell you when you’re active.

I paid but I’m still offline

Wait a few minutes, then turn your WiFi off and on. If you’re still offline, contact us with your M-Pesa confirmation message.

What if I renew before my plan ends?

Nothing is wasted. The new days are added on top of the time you already have.

Can I pay for someone else?

Yes. Enter the phone number on their account. The M-Pesa prompt goes to that phone, so its owner approves it.

I have a new phone. What do I do?

Connect the new phone to the WiFi and pay from the page that opens. It is linked automatically and the old phone is disconnected.
